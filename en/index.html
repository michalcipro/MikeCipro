Story

Expertise built on practice.

I have worked professionally in mental training since 2018, combined with coaching practice from the very start. In the first years I worked under the Brain Training brand, and in 2022 Milan Večeřa and I founded Winning Minds.

I hold international ISMCA and GPTCA (ATP) certifications, completed professional coach training accredited by the Czech Ministry of Education, and studied mental coaching in sport and business at university. We complement traditional coaching with stress diagnostics, breathwork and neurovisual training, so we are guided not just by feel, but by data.

Method

Less guesswork. More data.

At Winning Minds, Milan Večeřa and I have given mental training a clear structure. Every collaboration goes through three phases.

  1. 01

    Diagnostics

    We find out how you respond to stress and where your energy and focus leak away. That tells us where to start.

  2. 02

    A tailored plan

    Based on the results, we design training specifically for you: breathing, focus, self-confidence and pre-performance routines.

  3. 03

    Regular training

    We train continuously and keep re-evaluating progress. Mental form is built the same way as physical form.
